[B]About OneFrame[/B]
OneFrame adds role colors, player details and combat statistics to ESO's standard group and raid frames. Customize the information you see for tanks, healers and damage dealers.

[LIST]
[*]Custom role colors and optional sorting by role.
[*]Adjustable damage-shield color and opacity.
[*]Class icons, account names, and level or Champion Points.
[*]DPS or HPS counters, with a separate choice for each role.
[*]Thin Ultimate bars beneath group and raid frames.
[*]An optional estimate of total group DPS.
[*]Right-click actions to whisper, travel to a player or remove a member as group leader.
[/LIST]
